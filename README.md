## Data Protection & GDPR Compliance Checklist


## Overview
An audit of a fictional small nature conservation charity's (West London Wetlands 
& Parks Trust) data protection practices, benchmarked against the UK GDPR's seven 
core principles (Article 5) and the ICO Accountability Framework, covering donor 
data, volunteer vetting records, consent management, and breach response.

## Key Findings
- RAG-rated checklist across all 7 UK GDPR core principles plus charity-specific 
  areas (consent renewal, subject access requests, breach response, DPIA usage)
- 4 high-priority (Red) gaps identified: no data retention policy, unsecured 
  physical storage of event sign-in data, no subject access request (SAR) process, 
  and no breach response plan
- Additional Amber findings show a related pattern — consent and privacy notices 
  captured once but never reviewed, and broader-than-necessary internal access to 
  sensitive volunteer DBS vetting data
- Full audit report includes methodology, detailed findings, and prioritised 
  recommendations, scaled proportionately for a small (6-staff) organisation 
  rather than assuming resources of a large enterprise
